GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

When and where is DevFest Beirut 2026?

Saturday, November 28, 2026 at MEA Conference Center in Beirut’s airport district.

How do I attend?

The November 28 professional edition is free and invitation only. A confirmed invitation is required. Email info@gdglebanon.com to ask about invitations.

Who is the event for?

This exclusive edition is for industry professionals, including developers, tech leads and CTOs. A public edition will be announced later.

When is the public edition?

The public edition has not been announced yet. Its date and details will be shared later.

When will the agenda and speakers be announced?

The program is being finalized. Confirmed details will be shared by the organizing team.

How can my organization participate?

Explore the Partner and Sponsor options above, then email info@gdglebanon.com to discuss availability.
